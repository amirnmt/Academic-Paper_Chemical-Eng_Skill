# Phrase bank for engineering research articles

Sentence stems organized by section and by function (move). They are starting points: complete each with a specific fact, number, condition or citation, and choose stems whose strength matches your evidence. X, Y, Z stand for your content; [n] for citations; [value] for numbers.

Rules for use:
- Use a stem to start or link a sentence, then say something specific. A stem followed by a vague statement is padding.
- Vary stems only where the function changes. Keep technical terms identical throughout.
- Avoid decorative stems such as "plays a crucial role in", "highlights the importance of", "a pivotal step" (see `avoid-ai-signs.md`).
- Word lists for each function are in `vocabulary-by-section.md`. A larger general collection is the Academic Phrasebank of the University of Manchester (https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk/).
- Best of all: stems taken from recent papers in your target journal (`scripts/build_section_phrases.py`).
